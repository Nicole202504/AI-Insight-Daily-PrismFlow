8. **[Anthropic invests $100 million to train 10,000 engineers and tackle the enterprise AI talent gap](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-frontier-academy)**

   The first-of-its-kind Academy trains Frontier Deployed Engineers using the same standard of skills as Anthropic’s own engineers, starting with cohorts from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley, Novo Nordisk and others. Today we are launching Claude Frontier Academy to solve one of the most pressing issues in AI implementation: talent. Backed by a $100 million commitment, Anthropic aims to train 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers (FDEs) by the end of 2027. Engineers from Accenture, Bain, Capgemini, Commonwealth Bank of Australia, Deloitte, McKinsey, Morgan Stanley and Novo Nordisk are in the first cohorts. Every enterprise is racing to bring AI into its organization, but the people with the skills to make it work inside a real business have become the hardest talent to find. Inside the companies we work with, a small group of deeply skilled people drives an outsized share of what AI delivers. The Academy is the first of its kind from an AI company, built from our own work deploying Claude inside the world’s largest enterprises and the professional services organizations that serve them. “A small team of high-agency people with the right skills, access to Claude, and a deep understanding of how their business runs can transform an entire company. The hard part is building that depth of talent, engineers with the highest level of AI fluency who can take Claude from an idea to a system in production and turn it into faster processes and new products and services. No AI company has invested in developing that talent inside its customers and partners at this depth. Claude Frontier Academy trains people the way our own engineers learn, and we want those who graduate to set the standard for how AI gets built inside a business.” – Steve Corfield, Global Head of Business Development and Partnerships at Anthropic The first program trains 10,000 Frontier Deployed Engineers The Academy’s first program, the Frontier Deployed Engineer Residency, follows the medical model for building expertise. Doctors learn from practicing physicians, train on real cases, and are assessed before they practice alone. Likewise, FDE Residency engineers learn from practitioners, practice on realistic cases, and are assessed on their work before they earn the credential. The goal is agentic systems that change how a business runs, from faster, redesigned processes to new products and services. The program is for hands-on software engineers with strong fundamentals, a record of building with LLMs, experience helping others adopt AI, and a habit of tackling their business’s most important problems. Organizations nominate their strongest engineers, each arriving with a named Claude project to lead when they return. Prior experience building AI agents isn’t required. Engineers start with a multi-day in-person program with Anthropic engineers and licensed instructors. They work through a simulated enterprise deployment, from the right use case through security review to handover, and finish with a graded practical on a new scenario. Those who pass earn the Claude Resident Engineer badge and move into the 12-week residency, where they lead a real Claude use case at their own organization, supported by Anthropic engineers and learning from their cohort as they build. Engineers are assessed again at the end of the 12 weeks, and those who pass earn the Claude Frontier Deployed Engineer badge, with the first expected in early 2027. The first cohorts are running now Cohorts are running in San Francisco, New York and London. The program will grow toward 10,000 engineers by the end of 2027. Participation is by nomination; organizations can ask their Anthropic account team or Partner Account Manager whether they’re eligible. 9. **[Barclays scales Claude to upgrade operations and improve client experience](https://www.anthropic.com/news/barclays-scales-claude)**

   Barclays, the British universal bank, is expanding its strategic collaboration with Anthropic to integrate secure, enterprise-grade AI systems across its global operations. Barclays is extending Claude across the bank to accelerate software development, modernize legacy systems and improve operational efficiency. As part of this rollout, Barclays expects Claude Code adoption to reach 50% of its developer population by the end of 2026, rising to a majority of software engineers in 2027. Barclays builds with Claude to better serve customers Integrating AI across a large, complex and highly regulated organisation requires both capable technology and disciplined governance. Expanding its collaboration with Anthropic reflects a shared commitment to deploying AI responsibly, reliably, and in ways that deliver real outcomes, such as providing faster support for customers. “The true measure of any technology is the impact it has on customers, clients and colleagues, and the outcomes it enables,” said Anne Marie Darling, Group Co-Chief Operating Officer at Barclays. “Expanding the use of Claude across Barclays is about more than adopting AI. It is about transforming how work gets done: reimagining processes, enhancing customer and client experiences, and building a simpler, more efficient organization within a secure and well-governed environment. By reducing time spent on routine activities and streamlining decision-making, we can enable colleagues to focus their expertise where it matters most, solving more complex problems, accelerating innovation, and delivering better outcomes for our customers, clients and business.” “Software engineering and cyber security are both being reshaped by increasingly capable AI systems,” said Craig Bright, Group Co-Chief Operating Officer at Barclays. “We're moving towards AI as an increasingly agentic capability embedded within how we build, test, secure and operate technology. At Barclays, we're using Claude to help modernize legacy platforms, improve software quality, and allow our technical experts to focus on the most complex challenges. The combination of skilled engineers, cyber specialists and trusted AI capabilities has the potential to fundamentally improve how technology is developed and managed across large-scale enterprises.” Claude powers knowledge assistance to speed up customer servicing One example of the partnership already in action is Barclays’ Colleague Knowledge Assistant, which helps Barclays UK employees find information and answers more quickly when supporting their over 20 million UK retail customers. The capability, which has been live since 2025, is powered by Claude through a retrieval-augmented generation architecture. More than 16,000 colleagues have adopted the assistant, and it has handled over one million searches. The result is faster access to information and quicker support for customers. Enhancing operational efficiency in Barclays’ Global Markets business Within Barclays' Global Markets business, Claude models are helping streamline how client enquiries are processed and managed. Teams use Claude models to classify, enrich, and determine the optimal processing route for incoming emails, helping to prioritize and ensure requests contain the information needed for operations colleagues to take action. The platform processes approximately 120,000 emails each day, reducing manual handling activity while helping colleagues identify and act on relevant information more efficiently. Scaling AI responsibly As Barclays continues to expand its use of AI, responsible deployment remains central to the partnership. The bank applies robust governance, security controls and human oversight to AI use cases, ensuring technology is deployed safely and effectively across the organization. “Our work with Barclays is a significant milestone for us in the UK. Barclays serves millions of customers and clients across the globe, so their standard for AI is high. That's why Barclays is rolling Claude out with strong security and oversight,” said Paul Smith, Chief Commercial Officer at Anthropic. “Claude now helps 16,000 Barclays’ colleagues find answers for customers, sorts 120,000 emails a day, and will be in the hands of most Barclays engineers by 2027. Very few institutions hold AI to such a high standard.” Looking ahead The expanded partnership reflects Barclays’ long-term commitment to using AI to improve productivity, strengthen resilience, and improve customer and client outcomes. By combining Anthropic’s frontier AI capabilities with Barclays’ focus on responsible innovation, the organizations are working together to scale practical AI applications across one of the world’s largest financial institutions.
